Our Story

Serving the Bay Area
since 1995

SF Window Station was founded in 1995 with a simple belief: Bay Area homeowners deserve quality window and door installation from specialists who know local homes, local architecture, and local weather.

Over 30 years, we've grown into one of the region's most trusted window and door contractors, but our approach has never changed. Every project gets the same careful attention: we visit your home in person, take precise measurements, present clear options without pressure, and install with our own certified crews.

We work across every neighborhood in San Francisco and every major city on the Peninsula, from Victorian flats in the Richmond to mid-century homes in San Mateo.

Our Approach

No pressure.
Just expertise.

We don't run high-pressure sales operations. Our in-home estimate visits are consultations: we look at what you have, explain your options, and give you a clear written price. You decide if and when to proceed.

Our installers are not subcontractors. They are our own trained, certified employees who have been with us for years. When your job is scheduled, the same team who estimated it will install it.

  • Free in-home measurement and consultation
  • Written proposals with itemized pricing and product specs
  • Our own certified installation crews, no subcontractors
  • 3–4 week lead time from signed proposal to installation
  • Post-installation walkthrough with every job
  • Manufacturer warranty plus our own 4-year workmanship guarantee
