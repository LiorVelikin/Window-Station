Why Upgrade

A new door does more
than look beautiful

A quality door replacement improves your home in ways you'll feel every day — not just see. Bay Area homeowners who replace old doors consistently report measurable improvements in comfort, security, and energy bills.

  • Better thermal insulation — no more drafts from worn weatherstripping
  • Improved security with modern multi-point locking systems
  • Sound attenuation from street noise and Bay Area wind
  • Increased curb appeal and home resale value
  • Smooth, reliable operation — no more sticking or racking frames

Our Installation Process

Professional installation
in a single day

Our certified installers remove your old door, prepare the opening to exact specification, and install your new door system with proper weatherstripping, threshold, and hardware. Most door installations complete in 4–6 hours.

  • Free in-home measurement and product consultation
  • Written proposal with material options and firm pricing
  • Removal and disposal of old door included
