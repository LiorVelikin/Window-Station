Why Vinyl

The smart choice for
Bay Area homes

Vinyl (PVC) is the most popular window frame material in the United States — and for good reason. For Bay Area homeowners, vinyl's combination of insulating properties, zero-maintenance performance, and competitive pricing makes it the go-to solution for most replacement projects.

  • Excellent thermal insulation — vinyl frames don't conduct heat the way aluminum does
  • Never needs painting, staining, or sealing
  • Resistant to moisture, salt air, and coastal fog
  • Wide range of styles: double-hung, casement, slider, bay, awning, picture
  • Available in white, tan, grey, and custom colors
  • Energy Star certified options with Low-E glass and argon gas fills
Performance

Energy savings you
can measure

Modern vinyl windows with double-pane Low-E glass can reduce heat transfer dramatically compared to old single-pane aluminum windows — which were standard in thousands of Bay Area homes built in the 1950s through 1980s.

  • Up to 40% reduction in heating and cooling energy costs
  • Low-E coating blocks UV without reducing visible light
  • Argon gas-filled dual-pane units for enhanced insulating R-value
  • Noise reduction vs. single-pane glass (helpful near busy streets)
  • Condensation prevention — no more foggy interior glass in winter
