Millbrae FAQ

Common questions from
Millbrae homeowners

How fast can you complete a whole-house window replacement in Millbrae? +

For most single-story Millbrae ranch homes, a full-house window replacement (10–15 windows) is typically completed in a single day, occasionally two for larger homes. Because many homes here share similar opening sizes, we're often able to move efficiently from room to room. We'll give you a specific timeline estimate during your free in-home visit.

How long does window installation take? +

Most window replacement projects complete in a single day. Replacing 4–6 windows typically takes 4–6 hours; a full-home replacement of 10–15 windows usually takes 1–2 days. Lead time from signed proposal to installation is typically 3–4 weeks to allow for product ordering. Our crew cleans up completely before leaving.

Are there tax credits or rebates for new windows?+

Yes. Federal tax credits under the Inflation Reduction Act may apply for Energy Star certified windows and doors installed in your primary residence, worth up to 30% of product cost (up to $600 for windows). PG&E also offers utility rebates for qualifying upgrades. We'll advise you on which products qualify during your estimate.
