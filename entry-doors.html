Fiberglass Entry Doors
the smart upgrade

Fiberglass entry doors are the most installed entry door in the Bay Area for good reason. They never warp in the coastal fog, never swell in the rain, and can be stained to replicate the richness of natural wood — without any of the upkeep.

  • Available in smooth or realistic wood-grain texture
  • Energy Star certified insulated core options
  • Won't warp, crack, or splinter in coastal fog
  • Compatible with sidelites, transoms, and custom glass
  • 25+ color and stain options

French Double Entry
a statement entrance

Two-door French entry configurations create an impressive, wide entrance for homes with larger openings. They flood your foyer with natural light through divided glass panes while maintaining the security of a primary entry door.

  • Fiberglass or wood frame options
  • True or simulated divided light glass panels
  • Multipoint locking on both panels
  • Available as in-swing or out-swing
  • Sidelites and transoms available to extend the opening

Door Materials

The right material
for your home

Most Popular in Bay Area

Fiberglass

The best all-around entry door material for the Bay Area's coastal climate. Fiberglass withstands humidity, fog, and temperature swings while accepting paint and stain exactly like real wood.

  • Wood-grain texture available
  • Insulated core for energy efficiency
  • Won't rot, warp, or corrode
  • 25+ stain and paint options
  • Low maintenance — no annual repainting
Authentic Character

Wood

Real wood entry doors offer a warmth and authenticity that no other material replicates. The material of choice for Victorian and Edwardian homes where period accuracy matters, and for homeowners who love natural beauty.

  • Mahogany, alder, and Douglas fir species
  • Fully paintable and stainable
  • Period-accurate panel styles
  • Custom shop-built options available
  • Must be finished and maintained regularly
Maximum Security

Steel

Steel entry doors offer the highest security rating of any entry door material at the most affordable price point. Fire-rated options available. A practical, long-lasting choice for homes where security is the top priority.

  • Best in class security rating
  • Fire-rated options (common in SF multi-units)
  • Most affordable entry door material
  • Paintable in any exterior color
  • Dent-resistant steel skin options
