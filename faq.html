Cost & Pricing
How much does window replacement cost in San Francisco? +

Window replacement in San Francisco typically ranges from $400–$1,200 per window installed, depending on size, style, and frame material. Vinyl double-hung windows are the most affordable; fiberglass and wood cost more but offer superior aesthetics for historic homes. Specialty shapes (bay windows, large picture windows) are priced higher. A free in-home estimate from SF Window Station gives you an exact, itemized price for your specific project — with no obligation to proceed.

Do you offer financing for window replacement? +

Yes. We offer financing options for qualified customers, making it easier to replace multiple windows or upgrade your entire home at once. Ask about financing during your free in-home estimate and we'll walk you through available payment plans.

Are there tax credits or rebates for new windows? +

Yes. Federal tax credits under the Inflation Reduction Act may apply for Energy Star certified windows and doors installed in your primary residence. Credits can be worth up to 30% of the product cost (up to $600 for windows). PG&E also offers utility rebates for qualifying energy-efficient upgrades. We'll advise you on which products qualify during your estimate.

Timeline & Process
How long does window installation take? +

Most window replacement projects complete in a single day. Replacing 4–6 windows typically takes 4–6 hours; a full-home replacement of 10–15 windows usually takes 1–2 days. Our lead time from signed proposal to installation is typically 3–4 weeks to allow for product ordering and scheduling. You will not need to take time off work in most cases — our crew works efficiently and cleans up completely before leaving.

Do I need a permit to replace windows in San Francisco? +

In most cases, like-for-like window replacements do not require a permit in San Francisco. However, changing the size of the window opening, relocating a window, or making any structural alterations to the wall will trigger permit requirements. We assess your specific project during the free estimate and advise you clearly on permit requirements before any work begins.

What disruption should I expect during installation? +

Our installers protect your interior surfaces and floors before starting. Each window is typically exposed to the exterior for 20–60 minutes during removal and installation. There will be construction noise during the day. Most homeowners remain home during the project, and many work from home without issue. We leave the job site completely clean — no debris, no dust, no leftover materials.

Materials & Products
What is the difference between vinyl and fiberglass windows? +

Vinyl windows are the most popular choice: low maintenance, excellent thermal insulation, moisture-resistant, and budget-friendly. They do not require painting. Fiberglass windows cost more but offer greater dimensional stability (they don't expand and contract as much with temperature changes), can be painted any color, and are ideal for historic Bay Area homes that need a wood-like aesthetic without the upkeep. Visit our Vinyl Windows or Fiberglass Windows pages for more detail.

Are Energy Star windows worth it in the Bay Area? +

Yes, absolutely. Despite the Bay Area's reputation for mild weather, the combination of coastal fog, persistent winds, and wide temperature swings between morning and afternoon makes high-performance glass a meaningful investment. Energy Star windows with Low-E coatings and argon gas fills can reduce your heating and cooling costs by up to 40% compared to old single-pane or worn double-pane windows.

What window brands do you carry and install? +

We carry and install windows from five trusted manufacturers: Milgard (premium vinyl, fiberglass, and aluminum), Cascade (West Coast specialists), IWC (Independent Window Company, great for custom sizing), Simonton (Energy Star certified vinyl), and Windsor (wood and composite windows). During your free in-home estimate we'll recommend the best product for your home's style, performance needs, and budget.

Can you match the windows on my historic Victorian or Craftsman home? +

Yes. We specialize in historic Bay Area home styles including Victorian flats, Edwardian flats, Craftsman bungalows, and Spanish Mission homes. We can match historical profiles in fiberglass or wood, and we work in neighborhoods with strict design review guidelines including parts of the Mission, Noe Valley, and Pacific Heights. We'll advise you on exactly what's possible for your property during the in-home visit.
