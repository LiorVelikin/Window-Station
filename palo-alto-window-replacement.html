Palo Alto FAQ

Common questions from
Palo Alto homeowners

Do you have experience replacing glass in Eichler homes? +

Yes, Eichler glass replacement is a specialty of ours in Palo Alto. Eichlers rely on their post-and-beam structure staying intact, and the large glass panels and clerestory windows require careful handling to avoid disturbing that framing. We spec energy-efficient glass that keeps the original open, light-filled look while dramatically improving comfort and energy costs.

How long does window installation take? +

Every project is different, but we always give you a time estimate when we quote, and we’re usually accurate. We work as fast as the job allows without cutting corners. A proper installation takes the time it takes. If a project runs across multiple days, your home is always secured at the end of each day; we never leave openings exposed overnight. After your proposal is signed, product lead times typically run 3–5 weeks depending on what you’ve ordered. Our crew cleans up completely before leaving.

Are there tax credits or rebates for new windows?+

Yes. Federal tax credits under the Inflation Reduction Act may apply for Energy Star certified windows and doors installed in your primary residence, worth up to 30% of product cost (up to $600 for windows). PG&E also offers utility rebates for qualifying upgrades. We'll advise you on which products qualify during your estimate.
