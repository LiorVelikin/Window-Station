Aluminum Windows
San Francisco Bay Area

Modern aluminum windows combine ultra-slim frames with large glass openings and coastal durability. The preferred choice for mid-century modern, contemporary, and commercial properties across the Bay Area.

Why Aluminum

Sleek, strong, and
built for the coast

Aluminum is the material of choice for architectural homes that demand precision, minimal sight lines, and long-term performance near the ocean. Modern thermally-broken aluminum frames solve the old conductivity problem while maintaining aluminum's signature aesthetic advantages.

  • Ultra-slim profiles maximize glass-to-wall ratio and views
  • Corrosion-resistant — will not rust or degrade near the Bay
  • Powder-coated finishes in any RAL or custom color
  • Dimensionally stable — won't warp, swell, or shrink
  • Thermally broken frames available for improved insulation
  • Available in black, bronze, silver, champagne, custom colors
Best For

Ideal for modern
and mid-century homes

Aluminum windows are especially well-suited to certain Bay Area home styles where their thin lines and precise proportions complement the architecture rather than fighting it:

  • Mid-century modern homes in neighborhoods like Twin Peaks, Noe Valley, and the East Bay suburbs built in the 1950s–1970s
  • Contemporary renovations where maximizing natural light and outdoor views is the priority
  • Commercial and multi-unit buildings that require durable, long-lasting frames with minimal maintenance
  • ADUs and additions where a modern contrasting frame complements an older main structure
