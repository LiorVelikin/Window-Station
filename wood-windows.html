Why Wood

Authentic warmth
that no other material matches

Real wood windows have been the standard in Bay Area homes for well over a century — and for good reason. The richness of natural grain, the depth of a hand-applied finish, and the historical accuracy of period-correct profiles simply cannot be replicated by any engineered substitute.

  • Unparalleled visual warmth and natural character
  • Paintable and stainable — can be changed to match new exterior or interior finishes at any time
  • Required in some historic landmark properties and certain design review districts
  • Available in mahogany, Douglas fir, alder, and pine
  • Good thermal insulation when properly maintained and sealed
  • Can be repaired rather than replaced if sections are damaged
Maintenance & Care

What wood windows
require from you

Wood windows are a higher-maintenance choice than vinyl or fiberglass. They require periodic repainting or restaining every 5–10 years depending on exposure, and immediate attention if caulking cracks or paint chips to prevent moisture intrusion. For Bay Area homes with sheltered windows, this maintenance is minimal. For highly exposed windows on the ocean side of properties, some homeowners prefer clad wood (aluminum-exterior, wood-interior) for reduced external maintenance.

  • Inspect caulking and glazing compound annually
  • Repaint or restain exterior surfaces every 5–10 years
  • Address any paint failure immediately to prevent rot
  • Consider aluminum-clad exterior option for maximum weather protection
