Why Upgrade

Signs you need new
windows now

San Francisco's combination of coastal fog, thermal shifts, and century-old housing stock means windows take a beating. If you're noticing any of the following, it's time to call us:

  • Drafts or cold spots near window frames even when closed
  • Condensation or fog between double-pane glass panels
  • Difficulty opening, closing, or locking your windows
  • Visible rot, warping, or peeling on wood frames
  • Rising energy bills despite no change in usage
  • Street noise that's louder than it used to be

Frame Materials

Choose the right
frame material

Every home has different needs. We carry and install four primary window materials, each with distinct advantages for Bay Area homes and climates:

  • Vinyl — the most popular choice: low maintenance, excellent insulation, budget-friendly, and moisture-resistant
  • Fiberglass — premium durability with wood-like aesthetics; ideal for Victorian homes that need the look without the upkeep
  • Aluminum — slim frames, great for modern and mid-century homes; strong and corrosion-resistant near the coast
  • Wood — traditional warmth and character, often required in historic districts; we use premium species and finishes
