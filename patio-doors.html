Frame Materials

Choose the right frame
for your home

Lightweight & Strong

Aluminum

The go-to frame for modern homes and wide multi-panel configurations. Aluminum holds large glass panels without bulky frames, keeping sightlines slim and architectural.

  • Slim profiles, maximum glass area
  • Holds large multi-panel configurations
  • Powder-coated in any color
  • Corrosion-resistant — ideal near the coast
Best Value

Vinyl

The most accessible entry point for a patio door upgrade. Vinyl frames never need painting, resist moisture completely, and provide excellent thermal insulation year-round.

  • Zero maintenance — never paint or stain
  • Superior thermal insulation
  • Won't rot, warp, or corrode
  • Energy Star certified options available
Premium Choice

Fiberglass

The top-tier choice for Bay Area homes. Fiberglass is dimensionally stable — it doesn't expand and contract with temperature swings — and can be stained to look like real wood.

  • Wood-grain stain finish available
  • Exceptional thermal performance
  • Resistant to salt air and coastal fog
  • 50+ year expected lifespan
