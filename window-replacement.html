need replacing now

Window Styles

Every style we
replace & install

Most Requested in Bay Area
Casement Windows

Side-hinged and cranked open — casements give you a full, unobstructed opening for maximum ventilation. The most requested replacement window we install across San Francisco, Oakland, and Berkeley, especially in kitchens and living rooms facing the bay.

  • Full panel opening for maximum airflow
  • Excellent compression seal when closed
  • Single and double casement configurations
  • Vinyl, fiberglass, and wood-clad frames
Victorian & Edwardian Staple
Double-Hung Windows

Both the upper and lower sashes slide independently — a classic look that suits San Francisco's Victorian and Edwardian homes. Tilt-in sashes make cleaning easy from the inside, and period-accurate profiles are available to match historic millwork.

  • Both sashes tilt in for interior cleaning
  • Ventilate from top, bottom, or both at once
  • Period-accurate profiles for historic homes
  • Meets SF historic district window guidelines
Adds Space & Light
Bay & Bow Windows

Projects outward from the exterior wall to create a dramatic focal point and extra interior floor space. A defining architectural feature in San Francisco dining rooms, parlors, and master bedrooms — bay windows bring in light from three angles simultaneously.

  • Three-angle light exposure
  • Adds visual square footage to any room
  • Center picture pane flanked by casements
  • Custom window seat height options
Maximum Light & Views
Picture Windows

Fixed glass with no moving parts — picture windows deliver maximum light, unbeatable views, and the tightest energy seal of any window style. Ideal wherever your view matters most: living rooms, stairwells, and any space that frames the Bay.

  • No hardware to wear out over time
  • Best thermal performance of any style
  • Available in custom shapes and sizes
  • Pairs naturally with flanking casements or awnings
Wide Openings, Clean Lines
Sliding Windows

Panels glide horizontally on a precision track — a practical and modern choice for wide openings where there's no room for an outward-swinging sash. Common in contemporary Bay Area homes, condos, and kitchens above countertops.

  • No outward swing — ideal above counters
  • Smooth stainless steel roller hardware
  • 2-panel and 3-panel configurations
  • Integrated screen included standard
Fog & Rain Ready
Awning Windows

Hinged at the top and cranked open at the bottom — awning windows let in fresh Pacific air even during San Francisco's characteristic fog and drizzle. The natural choice for bathrooms, kitchens, and any room where you want ventilation regardless of weather.

  • Ventilate safely even in light rain
  • Excellent compression seal when closed
  • Often paired below large picture windows
  • Basement and clerestory applications
Frame Materials

Choose the right frame

Most Popular

Vinyl

The best value in Bay Area window replacement. Vinyl frames never need painting, resist moisture, and deliver excellent thermal insulation at the most accessible price point.

  • Zero maintenance — never paint or stain
  • Superior thermal insulation
  • Won't rot, warp, or corrode
  • Energy Star certified options
  • Wide color selection available
Premium Performance

Fiberglass

The premium choice for Bay Area's coastal climate. Fiberglass is dimensionally stable — it doesn't expand and contract with temperature changes — and can be stained to look like real wood.

  • Wood-grain stain finish available
  • Exceptional thermal performance
  • Resistant to salt air and fog
  • 50+ year expected lifespan
Authentic & Classic

Wood

Real wood frames offer a warmth and richness no other material can match. The standard choice for Victorian and craftsman homes — fully paintable, stainable, and compatible with historic preservation requirements.

  • Natural warmth and authentic character
  • Fully paintable and stainable
  • Period-accurate profiles available
  • Historic district compatible
Slim & Modern

Aluminum

Strong and slim, aluminum frames support large glass panels without bulky sightlines. The go-to material for modern and contemporary Bay Area homes, commercial projects, and any application where narrow profiles matter.

  • Slim profiles, maximum glass area
  • Handles large spans with ease
  • Powder-coated in any color
  • Corrosion-resistant near the coast

Energy & Savings

Cut energy costs
by up to 40%

Modern Energy Star certified windows can reduce heating and cooling costs significantly in Bay Area homes. With our fog, coastal winds, and temperature differentials, high-performance double or triple-pane glass makes a real difference year-round.

  • Low-E glass coatings block heat transfer while allowing full natural light
  • Argon-gas-filled panes dramatically improve insulating R-value
  • Federal tax credits may apply for certified energy-efficient replacements
  • Utility rebates available through PG&E for qualifying installations
