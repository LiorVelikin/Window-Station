8 signs your windows
need replacing now

  • Fog or condensation between double-pane glass (broken seal)
  • Drafts and cold spots even when windows are fully closed
  • Windows that stick, rattle, or won't lock properly
  • Visible rot, warping, or peeling paint on frames
  • Single-pane glass with no insulating properties
  • Heating or cooling bills that keep climbing
  • Increased outside noise entering the home
  • Windows over 20 years old with no prior replacement

Energy & Savings

Cut energy costs
by up to 40%

Modern Energy Star certified windows can reduce heating and cooling costs significantly in Bay Area homes. With our fog, coastal winds, and temperature differentials, high-performance double or triple-pane glass makes a real difference year-round.

  • Low-E glass coatings block heat transfer while allowing full natural light
  • Argon-gas-filled panes dramatically improve insulating R-value
  • Federal tax credits may apply for certified energy-efficient replacements
  • Utility rebates available through PG&E for qualifying installations
Window Styles

Window styles
we replace

Most Requested
Casement Windows

Opens outward on hinges for maximum ventilation and unobstructed views. The most popular replacement window in the Bay Area — especially in kitchens and living rooms.

  • Full opening for maximum airflow
  • Excellent weather seal when closed
  • Vinyl, fiberglass, and wood-clad options
Victorian Staple
Double-Hung Windows

Both sashes slide up and down independently — a classic look that suits San Francisco's Victorian and Edwardian homes perfectly. Easy to clean from inside.

  • Tilting sashes for easy cleaning
  • Period-accurate profiles available
  • Ventilate from top, bottom, or both
Adds Space & Light
Bay & Bow Windows

Projects outward from the wall to create a dramatic focal point and additional interior space. A defining feature in San Francisco dining rooms and parlors.

  • Adds floor space and architectural interest
  • Center picture pane with flanking casements
  • Custom seat-height options available
Maximum View
Picture Windows

Fixed glass with no moving parts — maximum light, unbeatable views, and the tightest energy seal of any window style. Ideal for framing Bay Area scenery.

  • No operating hardware to wear out
  • Best energy performance of any style
  • Pairs well with operable flanking windows
Wide Openings
Sliding Windows

Panels glide horizontally on a track — a practical choice for wide openings where there's no room for an outward swing. Clean, modern aesthetic.

  • No outward swing needed
  • Smooth stainless steel roller hardware
  • 2-panel and 3-panel configurations
Fog & Rain Friendly
Awning Windows

Hinged at the top and opens outward — lets fresh air in even during San Francisco's foggy, drizzly days. Perfect for bathrooms, kitchens, and basement levels.

  • Ventilate even when it's raining
  • Excellent energy seal when closed
  • Often paired below picture windows
